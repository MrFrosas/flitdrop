The honest answer first: yes, there is a Xender alternative with no ads. Flitdrop is a private, cross OS file transfer that shows no ads, bundles no ad trackers and never asks for broad permissions. Frequently asked

Is Xender safe?

Xender does move files locally, but it shows a lot of ads, bundles trackers and asks for broad permissions, and its transfers are not end to end encrypted, which is why many people are uneasy about privacy. If those concerns matter to you, Flitdrop is a private option with no ads, no ad trackers and end to end encryption on your own Wi-Fi.

Why does Xender show ads?

Xender is a free app that is funded largely by advertising, so the app displays banner and full screen ads and often promotes extra content to make money. Flitdrop is free too, but it has no ads at all because it runs as a desktop app on your own network rather than an ad supported service.

Is there a Xender alternative without ads?

Yes. Flitdrop is a Xender alternative with no ads and no ad trackers. It sends files, photos, links and clipboard between iPhone, Android, Windows, Mac and Linux over your local Wi-Fi, end to end encrypted, with nothing to install on the phone.

Is there a SHAREit alternative that is private?

Yes. SHAREit has faced criticism over ads and privacy, and Flitdrop is a private alternative: transfers go directly device to device over your local Wi-Fi, everything is end to end encrypted, there are no ad trackers and no account, so nothing is uploaded to a server.

Do I need to install an app?

Only the computer needs the Flitdrop app, which installs on Windows, Mac or Linux. The phone installs nothing: it just uses its browser as a lightweight web app after a one time QR scan, so there is no ad heavy app to keep on your phone.

Is Flitdrop free?

Yes. Flitdrop is free on your own network, with no subscription, no ads and no server cost for local transfers. You can download it from the official GitHub releases page.
