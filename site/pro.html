Questions

Is Flitdrop still free?

Yes. Flitdrop stays free, with every update: 30 files a day, up to 8 GB per file by default (16 GB in Settings), at full speed, end to end encrypted, with several phones. Texts and the clipboard do not count toward the 30 files. Pro is for people who send more, or who want the extra comforts.

What happens after 30 files in a day?

Files already on their way always finish. The next ones stay listed on the phone: they leave on their own after midnight, or as soon as you unlock Pro, while the Flitdrop page stays open on the phone; if you close it, choose them again later. The count starts again at midnight, your computer's time.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once and Pro stays unlocked for life, with every future update. Nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel.

Can I try Pro before paying?

Yes. Every computer gets 14 days of Pro to try, free, with no card and nothing to cancel. At the end, Flitdrop simply goes back to the free version.

How many computers can I use?

Up to 5 of your own computers with one purchase. On another computer, open Flitdrop, choose "I already bought it" and enter the e-mail you paid with. If you add a sixth, the computer that has gone longest without checking its licence goes back to the free version.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Within 14 days of your purchase, you get your money back without giving a reason, even after Pro is unlocked. Everything is explained on the refund page.

Who handles the payment?

Paddle, our reseller and merchant of record: Paddle takes the payment, handles taxes and sends your receipt. We never see your card details.

Does Pro need the internet?

Transfers never do. Pro needs the internet when you unlock it, then Flitdrop checks the licence by itself from time to time. Offline, Pro keeps working for at least two weeks after Flitdrop was last online; after that, Flitdrop goes back to the free limits until it is online again, and nothing is lost.
