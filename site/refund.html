How it works

  • Ask within 14 days of your purchase: with the button above, by writing to contact@flitdrop.com, or through Paddle, at paddle.net or with the link to manage your purchase in the receipt e-mail from Paddle.
  • Give the e-mail address used for the purchase and, if you have it, the order number on your receipt. You do not have to give a reason.
  • We confirm by e-mail, without delay, that we received your request, with its date and time. We then refund you through Paddle, with the payment method you used, within 14 days at the latest and usually within a few days. It costs you nothing.
  • Pro then switches off on your computers. Flitdrop keeps working with all its free features, and nothing on your computer is deleted.

Your legal right of withdrawal

If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you have 14 days from your purchase to withdraw, without giving a reason. For Flitdrop Pro this right does not end when Pro is unlocked: you can withdraw within 14 days of your purchase even after using Pro, and you get a full refund. To withdraw, use the "Withdraw from contract here" button above, Paddle's withdrawal button at paddle.net, the model form below, or any clear statement sent to contact@flitdrop.com or help@paddle.com. Where the law of your country gives you more, it applies.

After 14 days

After 14 days, a purchase is final, except where the law says otherwise, for example if Pro does not work as described. In that case, write to us: we fix it, or refund you if we cannot.

Mediation: if a written complaint to contact@flitdrop.com has not settled the dispute, you can refer it free of charge to the consumer mediator we have joined (articles L. 612-1 and following of the French Consumer Code): CM2C, Centre de la Médiation de la Consommation de Conciliateurs de Justice, 49 rue de Ponthieu, 75008 Paris, France, www.cm2c.net.

Who refunds you

Paddle.com Market Limited, our reseller and merchant of record, took your payment, so the refund goes through Paddle. When your request reaches us, we have Paddle refund you. You can also contact Paddle's buyer support directly at paddle.net to find your order, get your receipt or ask for a refund. The full terms are in the terms of sale and licence.
